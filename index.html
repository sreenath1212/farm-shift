Rice (Oryza sativa) - High-Vigor Cultivation Guidelines

Tailored for your registered plot

These recommendations are calibrated with agricultural university standards. Verify local field conditions prior to critical operations.

💧

Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD)

Install perforated field water tubes (pani pipe) to monitor field water level. Irrigate only when water depth drops to 15 cm below ground surface, saving 25% water.

🌾

System of Rice Intensification (SRI)

Transplant single young seedlings (8-12 days old) at wide square spacing (25 x 25 cm) with rotary cono-weeder inter-cultivation for maximal tiller density.

🧪

Targeted Soil Health & pH Buffering

In acid laterite/alluvial soils (pH 5.5 - 6.5), broadcast agricultural lime or dolomite at 250 kg/ha during primary tillage to unlock phosphorus fixation.

🍂

Biological Nitrogen & Fallow Enrichment

Broadcast Sesbania (Daincha) or Black Gram post-harvest to enrich organic carbon and fix biological atmospheric nitrogen (+35 kg N/ha).
