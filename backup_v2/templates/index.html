ℹ️ Earth Observation Data Limitations

• Optical NDVI measurements require cloud-free satellite passes (<20% cloud cover). During heavy monsoon spells, synthetic aperture radar (SAR) soil backscatter indices are substituted.

• Soil moisture measurements represent 0–7cm topsoil layer. Deep aquifer percolation is modelled via OpenET water balance equations.

• In-situ soil pH testing remains recommended prior to micro-nutrient soil dressing.
